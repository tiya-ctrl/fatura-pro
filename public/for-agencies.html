Common questions

Can more than one person work in the account?

Yes - up to five team members on the Business plan, all in the same account, with each invoice recording who created it.

Does it replace my accountant?

No. It provides expense and VAT summaries plus CSV exports that you can review with your accountant; it does not file tax returns or replace professional advice.

What happens to invoices we already sent?

Use a separate credit note that references the original invoice to keep a clear correction trail. The exact administrative requirements depend on your country and transaction, so check the rules that apply to you.
