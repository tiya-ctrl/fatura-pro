Common questions

Can more than one person work in the account?

Yes - up to five team members on the Business plan, all in the same account, with each invoice recording who created it.

Does it replace my accountant?

No, and it is not trying to. It produces the figures and the exports your accountant asks for, so the conversation starts from a spreadsheet rather than a shoebox.

What happens to invoices we already sent?

An issued invoice is never edited or deleted - you correct it with a credit note, which is what tax authorities expect and what keeps your numbering intact.
