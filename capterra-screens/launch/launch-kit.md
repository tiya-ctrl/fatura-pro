## 3. Indie Hackers (post on launch day)

https://www.indiehackers.com — add the product, then write a post in "Building in Public":

**Title:** I built an invoicing app for freelancers with international clients. Here's what I learned in the first month

**Post:**
A month ago I launched FaturaPro, an invoicing app for freelancers and small businesses whose clients are in other countries.

What I built: invoices in 5 languages and 18 currencies, WhatsApp and email reminders, receipt scanning and a quarterly tax summary.

What I learned so far:
1. Building is the easy part. Getting the first users is the real work, and content alone doesn't help when nobody follows you yet. I'm now talking to freelancers directly.
2. Sign-ups are not users. People sign up and never create a first invoice, so I'm working on making the first invoice take less than two minutes.
3. A free tool with no signup is a good way to be useful before asking for anything, so I built a free invoice generator.

I'd love feedback from anyone who invoices clients abroad: what tool do you use now, and what's missing?
